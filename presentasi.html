01 — Definition

So, what is Simple Present?

Simple Present Tense adalah tense yang digunakan untuk membicarakan kebiasaan, fakta, keadaan, dan kegiatan yang dilakukan secara rutin.

Example: I study English every day.
Saya belajar bahasa Inggris setiap hari.
Example: The sun rises in the east.
Matahari terbit di sebelah timur.
02 — Functions

When do we use it?

🔄 Habits

Kebiasaan yang dilakukan berulang.

I wake up at 6 a.m.

🌎 Facts

Fakta atau kebenaran umum.

Water boils at 100°C.

❤️ States

Perasaan, pendapat, atau keadaan.

She likes chocolate.

📅 Schedules

Jadwal yang sudah ditentukan.

The class starts at 7 a.m.

03 — Positive

Positive sentence formula

Subject + Verb 1 + Object

I / You / We / They

Gunakan verb dasar.

I play football every Sunday.

He / She / It

Tambahkan -s / -es.

She plays football every Sunday.
04 — Verb Rules

Don't forget the -s / -es

➕ Add -s

play → playsread → readswork → works

➕ Add -es

watch → watchesgo → goeswash → washes

He works in an office.
She watches movies every weekend.
05 — Negative

Make it negative

I/You/We/They + don't + Verb 1
He/She/It + doesn't + Verb 1

Don't

They don't play football.

Doesn't

She doesn't play football.

⚠️ Setelah doesn't, verb kembali ke bentuk dasar: doesn't play, bukan doesn't plays.
06 — Questions

Ask a question

Do + I/You/We/They + Verb 1?
Does + He/She/It + Verb 1?
Do you study English?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does she like coffee?
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
